Accessibility Recommendations

🎨 Don't Rely on Color Alone

Combine colors with text labels, icons, patterns, shapes or other visual indicators so information remains understandable without color perception.

🔳 Maintain Contrast

Make sure foreground content has sufficient contrast against its background and remains readable under different vision conditions.

🏷️ Use Clear Labels

Labels should communicate the meaning of an element independently from its color.

👁️ Test Multiple Profiles

A palette that works for one color vision condition may still cause problems for another.
